🎉 party, without the board

A made-up neighbourhood with its clubs, a street party, a book club and a family dinner. Everything you do is kept in this browser. All the UIs below share the same data, so answer something in one and see it in another.

🏠 Club HouseThe direction: fill-in-the-blank forms on the desktop, the swipe deck on phones (it opens by default there), and features built in: a calendar, chat (“Ask the board”), a beginner-friendly text mode, Mastodon-style keys (press ?), and a theme picker: Club House or Gazette. Desktop: forms · Phone: the deck · Themes: Club House, Gazette

The prototypes it came from

  • ClassicThe current local app: header links, pages and forms.Nav: top menu · Forms: classic fields
  • 💬 ChatA conversation with the board. Type “help”, or tap the chips.Nav: what you say · Forms: one question at a time
  • 🗓 CalendarThe month is the menu. Click a day to plan; drag an event to move it.Nav: the grid and its layers · Forms: a drawer with a day timeline
  • 🃏 DeckPhone-first. Swipe right to go, left to pass, up for maybe.Nav: bottom tabs · Forms: full-screen steps, a PIN pad
  • ⌨️ TerminalKeyboard only: j/k, y/m/n, ctrl-k to jump, :commands.Nav: keys and a palette · Forms: text buffers, :w to save
  • 📰 GazetteThe house newspaper. Reply slips and the press desk.Nav: a table of contents · Forms: sentences with blanks

You start signed in as Sam Rivera (the admin). The DEMO tab on the right edge of every UI switches person, signs out, resets the data, or starts an empty board (first-run setup). Every PIN is 1234.

Nothing leaves this browser: there is no email, no Mastodon and no publishing. Dates move forward each week so the demo stays current.
